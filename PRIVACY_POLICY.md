# Privacy Policy

**App:** ClearRoute X  
**Last updated:** October 9, 2026

This policy explains what ClearRoute X does with information. It is short because the app was built so that your information never reaches us.

## In short

- **No account and no servers.** The app works entirely on your device. There is nothing to sign in to.
- **We collect nothing.** No analytics, no advertising, no tracking. We never see your customers, readings, photos, reports or invoices.
- **Your data leaves only when you send it.** A report or an invoice goes out when you choose to share it, to the people you choose.
- **Two things use the network by themselves:** turning a street address into a spot on the map, and showing the map. Both are done by Apple on iPhone. Section 5 explains exactly what is sent.
- **Permissions are used only for what you ask.** Camera for photos, your photo library for photos you pick, and your location for the route and the map. You can refuse any of them.
- **You are in control.** You can edit and delete anything. Deleting the app deletes its data from the device. Because we keep no copy, we cannot recover it for you.

## 1. Who we are

ClearRoute X ("we", "us", "our") is an app for people who service swimming pools. It plans the day's route, helps with water chemistry, and makes service reports and invoices. It is published by ClearRoute X.

For privacy questions, contact us at **support@norfa.fun**.


## 2. What the app stores on your device

ClearRoute X keeps everything you enter in private storage on your own device. You create and control all of it:

- **Your business details:** business name, your name, logo, currency, tax rate, payment terms and the payment instructions you print on invoices.
- **Your customers:** names, phone numbers, email addresses, street addresses, gate codes, access notes, whether there is a dog, and a photo of the site if you take one.
- **Pools and spas:** size, surface, sanitizer type, equipment notes and target ranges.
- **Your schedule:** each customer's service day, and every stop on every day, including skipped and moved stops.
- **Visits:** water test readings, chemicals you add, the checklist, photos you take or choose, and your notes for the customer and private notes.
- **Reports and invoices:** the PDF files the app makes for you, and the invoice records: lines, discounts, tax, due dates and payments.
- **Your products:** the chemicals you carry, their strength and what you pay for them.

None of this is sent to us. The people whose details you store are your customers, so how you look after that information is your responsibility (see section 9).


## 3. What we collect

**Nothing.** We do not receive, store or process your information on any server. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party software that collects data. It does not read your contacts or your calendar.

The app keeps a small diagnostic log on your device that records technical errors only, never customer details. It stays on your device unless you choose **Settings, About, Share diagnostics** and send it to us to help fix a problem.


## 4. Permissions the app asks for

The app asks only when you use the feature that needs it, and works without the permission unless noted.

- **Camera.** To take before, after and issue photos at a stop, and a photo of a customer's site. Photos are saved inside the app.
- **Photo library.** To choose photos you want to add to a stop, or your logo. The app sees only the photos you pick, not your whole library.
- **Location, while the app is open.** To put the stops in the shortest driving order starting from where you are, and to show a blue dot on the map. Your location is used on your device for that moment. It is not stored and it is not sent anywhere by the app.

The app does not use your microphone, contacts, calendar, health data or notifications, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.


## 5. When information leaves your device

Only when you do something that sends it, or in the two cases below that the app does for you.

### When you send something

- You share or email a **service report** or an **invoice**. It goes to the people you choose, through the share sheet or your email app. After that, the recipient's own privacy practices apply.
- You tap **Navigate**. The address is handed to the maps app you pick (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze), whose privacy policy applies.
- You tap **Call**, **Text** or **Email** on a customer. The number or address is handed to your phone, messages or mail app.
- You share the diagnostic log.

### What the app does for you over the network

- **Finding an address on the map.** When you save or change a customer's address, the app asks the operating system to find its position. On iPhone that is Apple's geocoding service; on Android it is the system's location service. Only the **address text** is sent, with no name, no phone and no account, because the app has none. The position that comes back is saved on your device so the route and map can work. If you are offline the app tries again later.
- **Showing the map.** On iPhone the map is Apple Maps, which loads map tiles from Apple while you look at it. Apple's privacy policy applies to that.


## 6. Third-party services

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services. It relies on features built into your phone that Apple or Google provide, such as the share sheet, Mail, Maps, the camera, the photo picker and address lookup. Those features are governed by Apple's or Google's own terms and privacy policies.

The app is built with open-source software libraries. They run inside the app and do not send information anywhere.


## 7. Backups made by your phone

If you use iCloud Backup (iPhone) or Google backup (Android), your phone may include this app's data in the backup it makes of your phone, under Apple's or Google's terms. We do not control it, see it or hold it. It is also what lets you get your data back after losing or replacing your phone, so we suggest keeping it switched on.


## 8. How long information is kept, and deleting it

Information stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete a customer, a pool, a visit's photos, an invoice draft or a product inside the app. Customers can be archived instead, which keeps their history but takes them off routes and out of billing. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.

Because we keep no copy, we cannot restore anything that has been deleted, lost or damaged. Reports and invoices you have already sent stay with the people you sent them to.


## 9. Customers' information and your responsibility

If you use ClearRoute X for your work, the people you service have given you their details so that you can serve them. You decide what to keep, and you are responsible for following the privacy and consumer laws that apply to your business, for example telling customers what you keep and why. The app helps: you can find, edit, correct and delete a customer's information at any time.

Take care with gate codes and access notes. The app hides gate codes until you press and hold, but anyone who unlocks your phone can open the app, so use a passcode.


## 10. Children

ClearRoute X is a professional tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.


## 11. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act give people rights to see, correct, delete and move their personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to give you or delete. Everything the app stores is in your hands and can be edited or deleted inside the app. We do not sell or share personal information, because we never have it.


## 12. Security

Your information stays in the app's private storage on your device and is protected by your phone's own security. Use a passcode and keep your phone's software up to date. Because the app never sends your data to a server, there is no server for anyone to break into. A report or invoice is an ordinary file once you share it, so send it only to the people it is meant for.


## 13. International transfers

We do not transfer your information, because we do not receive it. If you use address lookup or maps, Apple or Google process the address text under their own policies, which may involve other countries.


## 14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version will be in the app and on our website with a new date at the top. If a change affects what happens to your information, we will say so when you update the app.


## 15. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your privacy: **support@norfa.fun**.
