## 3. Water chemistry guidance and safety

This section matters most, because pool chemicals can hurt people.

- **Suggestions, not instructions.** Doses, targets, the water balance index and the "Low, OK, High" markers are guidance. They follow published pool-care methods (including the widely used Trouble Free Pool "Pool Math" formulas) and use the strengths you enter for your own products. Real water, real products and real pools differ, and published methods themselves differ from one another. **You decide the final dose.**
- **Read the label.** Follow the manufacturer's label and safety data sheet for every chemical: protective equipment, handling, dilution, storage and disposal. If a suggestion in the App disagrees with a label, follow the label.
- **Never mix chemicals.** Add chemicals separately, one at a time, to circulating water, and never combine them with each other. Mixing some pool chemicals, for example chlorine products with acids, releases toxic gas. The App reminds you to add chemicals separately; that reminder does not replace training.
- **Test first, test after.** Dosing depends on accurate readings. Check that your test kit is fresh and that you read it correctly. Retest after adding chemicals, especially acid, because pH drifts.
- **Swimmers' safety.** The App cannot tell you when it is safe to swim. Follow local health rules and your own judgement before anyone enters the water.
- **Your inputs matter.** A wrong pool volume, product strength or reading gives a wrong dose. Check what you enter.
