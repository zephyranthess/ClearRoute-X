ClearRouteX

Route, water chemistry, reports and invoices for solo pool technicians

About the app

  • Today: the day's stops in order, with navigation, call and text.
  • Stop flow: water readings, dosing in your own products, checklist, photos and a service report in seconds.
  • Calendar and customers: a month at a glance, with every pool and spa you service.
  • Invoices: monthly invoices with tax, discounts, due dates and partial payments.
  • Private by design: everything stays on your phone. No account and no servers of our own.
